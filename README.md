# Ecommerce-Recommendation-Engine
🚀 Built a Customer Product Recommendation Engine Using SQL

I recently worked on a SQL-based Product Recommendation Engine using e-commerce customer, order, and product data.

🎯 Business Problem:
The objective was to recommend relevant products to customers based on their purchasing behaviour and preferences.

🔍 My Approach:

1. Data Analysis & Understanding
I started by studying the customer, order, and product data to understand purchasing patterns and identify possible recommendation strategies.

2. Category-Based Recommendations
I developed a recommendation approach based on product categories. For example, customers purchasing electronic products could be recommended other products from the electronics category.

3. Price-Based Recommendations
Next, I explored price-based recommendations by suggesting products priced similarly to or lower than the customer's previous purchase price.

4. Price Segmentation & Optimization
To make the recommendations more relevant, I divided product prices into four segments:

Low: ₹40 or below

Medium: Above ₹40 and up to ₹90

High: Above ₹90 and up to ₹150

Premium: Above ₹150

Using these price segments along with product categories, I refined the recommendation logic to make product suggestions more relevant to customers' purchasing behaviour.

💡 Key Learnings:

Understanding business requirements before writing SQL queries.

Analysing customer purchasing behaviour.

Applying category-based and price-based recommendation logic.

Using SQL concepts such as CTEs, joins, and CASE statements to structure recommendation logic.

This project helped me understand how SQL can be used not just for data analysis, but also to solve real-world business problems and support data-driven decision-making.
